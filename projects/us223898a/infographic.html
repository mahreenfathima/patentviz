The problem

Traditional lighting methods often lacked the reliability or brightness needed for consistent illumination. There was a need for a practical electric light source that could operate effectively.

How it works

  • An electric current is passed through a metallic filament.
  • The electrical resistance causes the filament to heat up.
  • The intense heat makes the metal glow brightly.
  • This incandescent glow produces visible light.
  • The lamp is powered by an external electrical source.

Why it matters

  • Provides a practical and reliable source of artificial light.
  • Enables illumination without the use of open flames or gas.
  • Establishes a foundational technology for modern electric lighting.
